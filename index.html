ℹ️ About TradeFinder

Empowering Retail Traders with Institutional Data

TradeFinder was born out of a simple necessity: to level the playing field. Retail traders often rely on lagging indicators and delayed news, while institutions execute trades based on real-time order flow and options data. Our platform bridges that gap.

We aggregate billions of data points from the National Stock Exchange (NSE) — including Options Chain structures, FII/DII activities, and sectoral rotation — and process them through our proprietary AI engine to present actionable insights.

Our Mission

To eliminate the "noise" of the stock market and provide traders with purely quantitative, bias-free, and high-probability trading setups. We believe data over opinions.

Note: TradeFinder is an educational platform. All AI scans, levels, and strategies are meant to supplement your own technical analysis, not replace it.
