📊 Understanding Option Chain

What is Open Interest (OI)?

OI represents the total number of outstanding contracts. In the Indian market, we look at **Put OI** as Support and **Call OI** as Resistance because institutions (the "Smart Money") are typically Option Sellers.

  • ✅ High PCR (> 1.2): Bullish Sentiment
  • ✅ Low PCR (< 0.7): Bearish Sentiment
  • ✅ OI Spike + Price Rise: Long Buildup

🔭 Sectoral Rotation Logic

Relative Strength (RS)

Money flows from one sector to another. By comparing a sector index (like Nifty Bank) against Nifty 50, we identify **Outperformance**.

💡 Pro Tip: Buy the strongest stocks in the strongest sectors. Avoid sectors in the "Red" zone of the heatmap even if they look "cheap".

🎯 The R-Factor Rule

Survival of the Disciplined

R-Factor is the ratio of your Profit to your Initial Risk. A trader with a 40% win rate can be highly profitable if their average R is > 2.0.

R > 3: Excellent R 2: Good R < 1: Avoid
